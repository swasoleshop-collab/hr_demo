Users and access

To add a person: in Supabase, open Authentication, then Users, then Add user, then Create new user. Enter their email and a temporary password, and tick Auto Confirm User. They then appear in this list as Not active. Click Edit, choose their role, and tick Account active. They must change the temporary password at first sign-in.

If someone forgets their password: delete them in Supabase (Authentication, then Users) and add them again with a new temporary password, then set their role here again.
